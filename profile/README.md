## Our direction

We work at the intersection of **AI architectures, computational autonomy, mathematical experimentation, and applied software engineering**. Our aim is to make useful research accessible, independently testable, and open to thoughtful collaboration.

We distinguish **implemented capabilities**, **research hypotheses**, and **independently validated results**. Code and documentation are starting points; reproducible experiments determine what works.

## Principles

**Inspectability** — Clear source code, test methods, and limitations.  
**Computational autonomy** — Local and independently operated options wherever practical.  
**Scientific discipline** — Claims should be tested against meaningful baselines.  
**Collaboration** — Credit contributors, welcome critique, and document progress.

## Work with us

We're interested in technical critique, reproducible benchmarks, thoughtful contributions, and research partnerships. Start by exploring a repository, opening an issue with a specific question, or proposing a testable improvement.

> **Axiom Zeta Innovations** — Build openly. Test rigorously. Improve together.

<sub>Individual projects may be prototypes or research-stage systems. Their presence here does not imply production readiness or independently verified performance claims. Review each repository's license and documentation before reuse.</sub>
